# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
TradeVision – Market analysis & paid trading signals platform.

## Users
- Anonymous visitor
- Free member
- Paid member

## Core Rules
- XAUUSD signal: visible to **any logged-in member**
- All other signals: **paid only**
- Live counters must be real-time and accurate

## Pages
- Home
- Markets Dashboard
- Blog
- Signals (paid)
- Post Detail

## KPIs
- Member conversion
- Paid upgrades
- Signal engagement

## Non-goals
- No trading execution
- No broker integration
